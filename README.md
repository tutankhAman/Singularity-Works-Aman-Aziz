**Lucid** sits in on your calls and tells you what you missed. Real-time mic and system audio get fed through an LLM, which flags conflicts, decisions, follow-ups, and blind spots the moment they land. Alerts hit a HUD overlay while you're still talking. Post-call, the full breakdown lands in your inbox. Meetings go Active. Documents go Learning. Your brain stays ahead.
